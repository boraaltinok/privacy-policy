## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no login and no password.

### How do I find a recipe?

Use the search field on the home screen, or tap a category (for example Main Dishes, Snacks, Desserts) or a diet filter (for example High Protein, Vegan, Gluten Free, Keto). **Simple Recipes** shows recipes with simple ingredients that are easy to follow. **View AirFryer Cheat Sheet** opens a table of temperatures and times.

### What does a recipe show?

Temperature, preparation and cooking time, servings, difficulty, ingredients, step-by-step instructions and estimated nutrition numbers per serving (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber). **Start Cooking Mode** shows one step at a time.

### My food was not done, or too dark.

Air fryers differ. Use the time and temperature as a starting point, do not overfill the basket, shake or turn the food, and check that it is cooked through. The cheat sheet has tips.

### Are the nutrition numbers exact?

No. They are estimates and change with brands and amounts. They are not medical or dietary advice. If you have an allergy, always read the full ingredient list: a diet label is not a guarantee. See the [Disclaimer](https://github.com/boraaltinok/privacy-policy/blob/main/air-fryer-recipes-disclaimer.md).

### How do I save a recipe?

Tap the heart. Favourites are stored on your phone and are not synced between phones. Deleting the app deletes them from the phone, but your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may keep them and bring them back.

### How do I change the language?

**Settings › Language.** "Auto" follows your phone's language.

### What do I pay for?

The app is free to download; the recipes are part of **Premium**. You can choose **Monthly**, **6 Months** (with a 1-week free trial if your store account is eligible) or **Lifetime** (pay once). Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: **Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions**. On Android: **Google Play › profile picture › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions**. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. After you cancel, Premium stays until the end of the period you paid for.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app on the plan screen and tap **Restore Purchases** while signed in to the store account you bought with (Apple Account or Google account).

### How do I get a refund?

On iPhone, Apple handles refunds: ask at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). On Android you can ask Google Play (see [Google Play's refund page](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)) or write to us at [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software).

### The plan screen shows no prices.

Prices load from the App Store or Google Play. Check your internet connection and tap **Retry**.

### Recipes or pictures do not load.

Check your internet connection, then close and open the app. The app needs a connection to load recipes and pictures.

### What does the app know about me?

No name and no email. The app gets a random user ID. RevenueCat (purchases) stores what it gets under that ID; on iPhone, Amplitude (usage statistics) does too. Crash reports and Google Analytics use other random IDs. Apple or Google Play keeps your purchases under your store account. On iPhone usage statistics are on. On Android they are off if the country in your phone's language setting is in the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, the UK or Switzerland, or if that country is unknown; otherwise they are on, and only Google Analytics receives them. The Android app sends nothing to Amplitude. The app shows no ads. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/boraaltinok/privacy-policy/blob/main/airfryer-rezepte-app-privacy-policy.md).

### How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone and to stop any further collection. To have the records stored under your user ID deleted (sign-in record, purchase record at RevenueCat and, on iPhone, usage statistics at Amplitude), tap **Settings › Contact Us** and write "delete my data". The subject line carries your user ID. We delete them within 30 days.

Settings opens only with Premium, or while the app is open without payment. If you never bought Premium, you may not be able to open it, and then the app cannot show you your user ID. Without it we cannot find your records. You may still write to [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software), and we will help as far as we can.

Crash reports and Google Analytics records are not stored under your user ID, so we cannot pick yours out. Google Analytics records are kept for at most 14 months; crash reports are deleted after Google's own keep-time.

### I found a mistake in a recipe, or I have an idea.

Email [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software) with the recipe's name and what was wrong. A screenshot helps.
