# Bookdays - Support

Welcome to the Bookdays support page. We're here to help you keep your reading diary safe and easy to use.

## Getting Started

Bookdays is a reading diary for iPhone and iPad. Your books stand in a bookcase, and each book opens to its own diary page where you keep what you thought while reading.

### Key Features
- **Your bookcase**: scan a barcode, search, snap your copy, or pick from a "reading now" list to add books
- **A diary page for every book**: type a thought, photograph a page to keep a quote, or hold to talk
- **Calendar and year card**: see your reading month by month and share a card of your year
- **Reading room**: calm ambient sounds while you read
- **Import and export**: bring your list from Goodreads, StoryGraph, Bookmory or a CSV file, and export everything as CSV, ZIP or Markdown
- **iCloud backup and sync**: free and automatic, in your own iCloud

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need an account?**
A: No. Bookdays has no sign-up and no login. Your diary is tied to your device and your own iCloud.

**Q: Where is my diary kept? Is it private?**
A: On your device and in your own iCloud, which we can't read. Book lookups and voice notes go through our server, as described in our [Privacy Policy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/boraaltinok/privacy-policy/main/bookdays-privacy-policy.md). Analytics and crash reports never contain your books or your writing.

**Q: What's free?**
A: 3 books, 1 page-photo quote and 1 voice note, the bookcase with one label, and the reading room with Rainy window. Backup, sync, export, and seeing or editing your diary are always free. You can check the current details in **Settings › Help › What's free?**

### Adding Books

**Q: My barcode scan didn't find the book. What can I do?**
A: Some books, especially new or regional editions, aren't in the public catalogues yet. Use **Snap your copy** to photograph the cover, or type the title. If you're offline, the scan waits and tries again later.

**Q: Where does book information come from?**
A: From public catalogues: Open Library, the Deutsche Nationalbibliothek, the Bibliothèque nationale de France and the National Diet Library. You can edit any detail on your own shelf.

**Q: I deleted a book. Did I get a free slot back?**
A: No. The free limit counts every book added over the life of your diary, so deleting doesn't give a slot back.

### Capturing Thoughts

**Q: How do page-photo quotes work?**
A: Photograph the page, then drag over the lines you want to keep. The text is read on your device and the photo is discarded right away; only the lines you keep are saved.

**Q: How do voice notes work?**
A: Hold to talk and release when you're done (up to 3 minutes). The recording is sent through our server to OpenAI, which writes it down. We don't keep the recording. The first time, Bookdays asks for your permission. You can turn voice notes off in **Settings › Voice notes**.

**Q: My voice note says "Try again later" or is still waiting.**
A: Your recording is safe on your phone. Notes recorded offline are written down when you're back online. There is also a fair-use limit of 60 minutes per 30 days, the same for everyone; when you reach it, notes wait and are written down later. You can always type in the meantime.

**Q: Hold to talk isn't available in my language.**
A: Voice notes are enabled language by language once they reach our accuracy bar. Typing and page-photo quotes work in every language.

### Permissions

**Q: Why does Bookdays need the camera?**
A: To scan a book's barcode, snap its cover, photograph a page for a quote, or photograph an object for your shelf. Photos are processed on your device.

**Q: Why does Bookdays need the microphone?**
A: Only for Hold to talk. If you never use voice notes, you can keep the microphone off.

**Q: I denied a permission. How do I turn it back on?**
A: Go to **iOS Settings › Bookdays** and turn on Camera, Microphone or Notifications.

### iCloud Backup and New Devices

**Q: Is my diary backed up?**
A: Yes, if iCloud is on for Bookdays. You'll see "Backed up to your iCloud" in **Settings › Backup & export**. If it says backup is off, turn on Bookdays in **iOS Settings › your name › iCloud**.

**Q: How do I move to a new phone?**
A: Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud on, then open Bookdays: your shelf comes back by itself. Using a different Apple ID? Export as CSV in **Settings › Backup & export**, then import it on the new phone.

**Q: My iCloud is full.**
A: Your diary stays safe on your phone, but backup pauses until there is space. Free up iCloud storage or upgrade your iCloud plan, and backup resumes on its own.

### Import and Export

**Q: How do I import from Goodreads, StoryGraph or Bookmory?**
A: Go to **Settings › Backup & export › Import books** and follow the steps to export a file from the other app and pick it. The file is read on your device.

**Q: How do I export my diary?**
A: In **Settings › Backup & export**, choose CSV or Markdown. Export is always free.

### Subscription & Bookdays Plus

**Q: What do I get with Bookdays Plus?**
A: Unlimited books, page-photo quotes and voice notes, every shelf style and decoration, and all reading sounds.

**Q: How does the free trial work?**
A: The yearly plan includes a 1-month free trial for eligible new subscribers. If you started the trial and allowed notifications, we remind you before it ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

**Q: How do I restore my purchase on a new device?**
A: Go to **Settings › Plan › Restore purchases**, signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Go to **Settings › Plan › Manage subscription**, or **iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions**. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

**Q: What happens to my diary if my subscription ends?**
A: Nothing is lost. You can still see, edit, back up and export everything. Adding past the free limits and Plus features are locked again.

**Q: How do I get a refund?**
A: Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Technical Support

**Q: The app is not working properly. What should I do?**
A: Try these steps:
1. Close and reopen the app
2. Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store
3. Check your internet connection (needed for book lookups, voice notes and sync)
4. Check that iCloud is on for Bookdays in iOS Settings
5. Restart your device

## Data Management

**Q: How do I delete my data?**
A: Deleting the app deletes the diary on your device. To delete the iCloud copy: **iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Bookdays**. Once you delete the iCloud copy, Bookdays pauses backup and won't upload it again on its own.

**Q: Can you delete the data you hold about me?**
A: We hold no diary content. Our server only keeps pseudonymous counters (for example voice-note seconds per day) for a limited time. To ask us to delete them, or your purchase records at RevenueCat, email us from **Settings › Help › Contact**: the email includes your Support ID so we can find them.
