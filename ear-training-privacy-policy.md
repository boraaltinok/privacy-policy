# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **NEXTLINE YAZILIM LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ** ("we," "us," or the "Service Provider") handles information in connection with the mobile application **Ear Training: Play by Ear** (the "Application"). The Application is a gamified music-education app for practicing interval, chord, and scale-degree recognition by ear.

By using the Application, you consent to our collection, use, and disclosure of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Data Stored Only on Your Device

The Application is designed to work fully offline for its core content. Your training data is created and stored **locally on your device** using an on-device database (Hive) and never leaves your device through us. This includes:

- Lesson, unit, and level progress; stars and best-accuracy scores
- Daily streak counts and history
- Practice statistics, assessment scores, and "misleading pair" confusion data
- Your settings (note-name mode, reminder time, instrument voice, appearance)

We do not have a backend server, user accounts, or cloud database for your training content. This on-device data is not transmitted to us and is removed when you delete the Application. (Uninstalling the Application permanently erases this local data; it is not backed up by us.)

### Information We Do Not Collect

The Application does **not** provide account registration or login, and does **not** ask you to create a profile. We do **not** collect your name, email address, or password through the Application, and we do **not** access your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, location, or health data.

### Automatically Collected Information

To operate, improve, and support the Application, certain information is collected automatically through the third-party services listed in Section 3:

- **Pseudonymous identifier.** The Application generates a persistent, pseudonymous user identifier so that analytics, diagnostics, and purchases can be associated with an anonymous device/session. This identifier is an anonymous RevenueCat identifier, or where that is unavailable, a locally generated random UUID stored on your device. There is no login or account, and the identifier is not tied to your real-world identity.
- **Usage and product-analytics events.** Anonymous events describing how the Application is used (for example: onboarding steps completed, lessons finished, accuracy and star outcomes, paywall shown, trial started, purchase made). These events are associated with the pseudonymous identifier above.
- **Diagnostic and crash data.** If the Application crashes or encounters an error, diagnostic information (device model, operating-system version, and a crash stack trace) is collected to help us fix problems.
- **Purchase and subscription data.** Subscription status, transaction/purchase history, and trial eligibility, processed by our purchases provider to unlock premium features and restore purchases.

## 2. Advertising and Tracking

The Application does **not** display third-party advertising and does **not** contain advertising SDKs.

The Application does **not** track you across other companies' apps or websites, does **not** access the device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and does **not** build advertising profiles or serve you ads. Because it does not track you, it does not present Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The analytics and diagnostics described in this policy are pseudonymous product analytics used only to operate and improve the Application. We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the Application schedules **local practice reminders** (and, if you start a trial, a one-time reminder before the trial ends) directly on your device using local notifications. These are generated on-device and are not sent from a push server; we do not maintain a push-notification token for you on a backend. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or in the Application's Settings.

## 5. Opt-Out Rights
