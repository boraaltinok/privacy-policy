# Ear Training: Play by Ear — Support

Welcome to support for **Ear Training: Play by Ear**, a gamified daily app for learning to recognize scale degrees, chords, and progressions by ear. This page answers common questions and tells you how to reach us.

---

## Getting Started

- **Compatibility:** iPhone and iPad running iOS 15.0 or later.
- **How it works:** Complete the onboarding ear assessment, which places you at your starting lesson. Practice short daily sessions, keep your streak, and track your accuracy over time. Core training works fully **offline** — no account or sign-up required.
- **Voices:** Practice with a piano voice for free; guitar and bass voices are included with Premium.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Do I need an account to use the app?**
No. There is no login or sign-up. Your progress is stored privately on your device.

**Is my data private?**
Your lessons, progress, streaks, and stats are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us. We use anonymous analytics and crash reporting to improve the app. See our Privacy Policy for details.

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes. All training features work without an internet connection. A connection is only needed to make or restore a purchase, load subscription options, and send anonymous analytics.

### Technical Support

**The audio isn't playing or sounds delayed.**
Try these steps:
1. Make sure your device volume is up and not in Do Not Disturb.
2. Note that lessons play through the silent switch by design (media playback).
3. If using Bluetooth headphones, reconnect them; the app will re-play the current question after a route change.
4. Close and reopen the app.
5. Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store.

**My streak reset.**
Streaks are activity-based: missing a full day resets the current streak. Your longest streak and overall progress are preserved.

**I switched devices or reinstalled — my progress is gone.**
Progress is stored locally on each device and is not backed up by us, so it does not transfer automatically. Your **subscription** can be restored (see Billing below). Cloud backup of progress is being considered for a future update.

### Billing and Subscription

**What does Premium include?**
The full method path (all degrees, chords, and progressions), unlimited sessions, advanced mastery stats, and all instrument voices (piano, guitar, bass).

**How much does it cost?**
Monthly is $6.99/month and Yearly is $29.99/year. The Yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Prices shown at purchase reflect your region and currency.

**How do I cancel or manage my subscription?**
Manage or cancel any time in your Apple App Store account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the current period, then return to the free experience with your progress preserved.

**How do I restore a purchase?**
Use the restore option in the app while signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

**How do I get a refund?**
All purchases are handled by Apple. Refund requests are made through Apple's App Store, not directly through us.
