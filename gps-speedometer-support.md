# Speedometer: GPS Speed & HUD - Support

Welcome to the Speedometer support page.

## Getting Started

Speedometer shows your current speed from GPS in one large, honest readout. Pick your unit and vehicle mode, allow location access, and the speed appears as soon as the GPS has a fix. Premium adds HUD mode for the windshield, trip recording with history, a speed alert, a Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Live Activity, and extra dial styles. The app is available for iPhone and iPad.

### Key Features
- **Live GPS speed**: a big readout in mph, km/h, or knots, with an accuracy indicator. It never shows a fake number: if there is no GPS fix, it says so.
- **Vehicle modes**: car, bike, motorcycle, boat, and running, which tune how the GPS is read.
- **HUD mode** (premium): mirrored readout to reflect on the windshield at night, with brightness boost and auto-dim.
- **Trips** (premium): record distance, max speed, average speed, duration, and a route outline. History stays on your device.
- **Speed alert** (premium): set a threshold and get a sound, vibration, or both when you go over it.
- **Live Activity** (premium): current speed, distance, and elapsed time on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while a trip is recording.
- **Private by design**: no account, no server. Your location and trips never leave your phone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Location and GPS

**Q: Why does Speedometer need location access?**
A: Speed is calculated from GPS position changes, so location is the whole app. Speedometer asks for "While Using the App" access, never "Always". Everything is processed on your phone and nothing is uploaded. You can review the permission in Settings > Speedometer > Location.

**Q: Why does it ask for Precise Location?**
A: Approximate location is only accurate to a few kilometers, which is useless for speed. Speedometer needs Precise Location to work. If it is off, the app shows a screen explaining this and offers to turn it on for the session or to open Settings.

**Q: It says "Waiting for GPS". What should I do?**
A: The phone has not got a satellite fix yet. Move outdoors or near a window, keep the phone where it can see the sky, and give it up to a minute. GPS does not work indoors, in tunnels, or in underground car parks. Waiting time never counts against the free allowance.

**Q: The readout shows 0 while I am moving.**
A: A steady 0 with a good GPS state means the GPS is reporting you as stationary. This can happen for the first few seconds of movement or when the phone is somewhere with a weak signal. Give it a moment and make sure the phone is not buried in a bag or under the dashboard.

**Q: Why does the app read lower than my car's dial?**
A: Most vehicle speedometers are set to read a little high on purpose. GPS measures ground speed directly, so it usually shows a few units lower than the dial. Settings > Help > "Why does my car read higher?" has the full explanation.

**Q: Why is the compass heading blank?**
A: The heading cell uses the phone's compass and falls back to your direction of travel while moving. If the phone cannot get a compass reading, the cell stays blank until you move.

**Q: iOS shows a blue location indicator. Is the app tracking me?**
A: The blue indicator appears only while a trip is being recorded, because location keeps updating in the background so the trip and the Live Activity stay live. Stop the trip and it goes away. Outside of a recording, the app does not use location in the background, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

### HUD Mode

**Q: How do I use HUD mode?**
A: Open HUD from the dashboard (premium). Place the phone flat on the dashboard at night and the mirrored readout reflects on the windshield. Brightness boost pushes the screen to maximum; auto-dim lowers it when the app is idle. Both can be changed in Settings > Display.

**Q: The screen keeps turning off.**
A: Turn on "Keep screen awake" in Settings > Display. It keeps the readout and HUD on while the app is in front.

### Trips

**Q: How do I record a trip?**
A: Tap Record on the dashboard (premium). The trip keeps recording if you lock the phone or switch apps. Tap Stop in the app or in the Live Activity to end it. The summary shows distance, max, average, duration, moving time, and a route outline.

**Q: Where are my trips stored? Can I export them?**
A: Trips are stored only on your phone. There is no cloud sync, no account, and no export in this version. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Q: How do I delete a trip?**
A: Swipe a trip in the list, or open it and tap Delete. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

**Q: The app was closed while recording. Is my trip lost?**
A: No. An open trip is saved as it goes. When you reopen the app it offers to recover the trip.

### Speed Alert

**Q: How does the speed alert work?**
A: Set a threshold from the dashboard (premium). When your speed goes over it, the app plays a chime or beep, vibrates, or both. Choose the sound in Settings > Alerts. The alert is a convenience and should never replace watching the road and your instruments.

### Live Activity

**Q: The Live Activity does not appear.**
A: Live Activities need iOS 16.1 or later, a recording trip, and Live Activities enabled in Settings > Speedometer > Live Activities. On phones without a Dynamic Island, it appears on the Lock Screen only.

### Free Readout, Subscription, and Premium

**Q: How much of the app is free?**
A: The live readout is free for a total of 30 seconds across all sessions. Waiting for GPS, onboarding, settings, and the paywall do not count. After that the readout locks until you subscribe or buy Lifetime. HUD, trips, alerts, the Live Activity, and dial styles are premium from the start.

**Q: I reinstalled the app and the readout is still locked.**
A: The free allowance is a one-time allowance per device and is kept when the app is reinstalled. This is by design, not a fault. Restore Purchases if you already have premium.

**Q: What plans are there?**
A: Weekly, Yearly with a 3-day free trial, and a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency. Yearly is pre-selected because it is the best value; you can pick any plan.

**Q: How does the free trial work?**
A: Start the Yearly plan and the first 3 days are free. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged. On the second day of the trial the app can send you one reminder notification that the trial is ending, if you allowed it. Cancel any time in your App Store subscription settings.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings > Manage subscription in the app, or open the Settings app > your name > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

**Q: How do I restore my purchase on a new device?**
A: Open Settings > Restore Purchases (also on the paywall). Sign in with the same App Store account you bought with. If the app says there is nothing to restore, the purchase was made with a different Apple account.

**Q: The paywall says prices could not load.**
A: The app needs an internet connection to load prices from the App Store. Check your connection and tap Retry. The app never shows a guessed price.

### Battery and Heat

**Q: The app uses a lot of battery.**
A: Continuous GPS at full accuracy, a bright screen, and keep-awake all draw power. This is normal for any GPS speedometer. Use a car charger for long drives. If iOS reports the phone is getting hot, the app lowers its GPS rate automatically until it cools.

### Technical Support

**Q: The app is not working properly. What should I do?**
A: Try these steps:
1. Check that location permission is "While Using the App" and Precise Location is on
2. Move outdoors with a clear view of the sky
3. Close and reopen the app
4. Restart your device
5. Make sure you have the latest app version

**Q: How do I update the app?**
A: Updates are usually automatic. You can also check the App Store manually.

## Data Management

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Speedometer has no server and no account. Your location, speed, trips, and settings stay on your phone. The only service the app talks to is RevenueCat, which handles purchases together with Apple. This version sends no analytics and no crash reports. See the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/boraaltinok/privacy-policy/blob/main/gps-speedometer-privacy-policy.md).

**Q: How do I delete my data?**
A: Delete trips inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data. The only thing that can remain on the device is the free-seconds counter (a number from 0 to 30) kept in the iOS Keychain. It contains no personal information.
