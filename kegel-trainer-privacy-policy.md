# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-07_

Thank you for using **Reps** (App Store name: "Kegel Exercises for Men: Reps", the "Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how NEXTLINE YAZILIM LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ ("Service Provider", "we", or "us") handles information when you use the Application.

The Application is a guided pelvic-floor (kegel) training program for men: an 8-week plan of short daily sessions with vibration, tone, or voice cues, step-by-step guides, a weekly self-timed hold test, and educational content linked to its sources. **There is no account, and your training information stays on your device.** We do not run a server that stores your data, and we never receive your goal, your answers, your hold times, or your training log.

## 1. Information We Handle

### Stored Locally on Your Device (never sent to us)

Everything you enter or create while training is saved only in the Application's local storage on your device. We never receive, see, or store any of it. This includes:

- **Your focus and setup answers**: the primary goal you pick, your experience level, your answer to the "Before you start" safety question, your training pace, and the daily moments you anchor your sessions to.
- **Training log**: each completed session (date and time, plan week and day, position, exercise counts, duration, the cue pack used, and whether the screen was locked).
- **Hold tests**: your self-timed hold results, the date of each test, and your weekly check-in answers (for example, how easy the muscle was to find and how the sessions felt).
- **Plan and streak**: your plan progress, streak, checkpoints, and graduation status.
- **Reminders and settings**: reminder times, the reminder text and emoji you choose, cue and sound settings, appearance, the Face ID lock and app-switcher cover settings, and your privacy choices.

This information lives only in the Application's on-device storage. Deleting the Application deletes it. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this local data may be included in that device backup according to your Apple settings; that backup is managed by Apple, not by us, and is not a sync to our servers. Restoring a purchase brings back your subscription, not your training log.

### Anonymous Usage Analytics (only if you allow it)

During setup the Application asks whether you want to share anonymous usage. **Google Analytics for Firebase stays switched off unless you tap "Allow".** Until you answer, usage events are held only in the Application's memory and are discarded if you choose "Not now". You can change your choice at any time in **Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage**; turning it off stops collection and resets the analytics identifier on your device.

If you allow it, the Application sends anonymous product events such as: which onboarding step was viewed, that a session started, was paused, or was completed, which cue pack was used, that a hold test was completed (not its result), which paywall was shown, the plan type selected (yearly or lifetime), whether a purchase or restore succeeded, whether you allowed reminders, and which Learn or how-to topic was opened. A plan status (free, trial, or Reps Plus) may be attached as a user property.

The Application uses a strict allowlist so that the following can **never** be sent to analytics: your primary goal, your safety-question answer, your hold times or test tiers, your check-in answers, your training log, or your reminder text.

Advertising features of Google Analytics are disabled: ad storage, ad personalization, and ad user-data signals are turned off, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not collected, and automatic screen reporting is disabled. As with any network service, Google may derive an approximate location (such as country or region) from your IP address.

### Crash Reports

If the Application crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report containing the stack trace, device model, iOS version, Application version, a Firebase installation identifier, and the names of the last few in-app actions before the crash (action names only, with no values or training data). Crash reports are on by default and can be turned off in **Settings › Privacy › Send crash reports**.

### Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and tracked by RevenueCat. RevenueCat assigns a random, **anonymous App User ID** (the Application never links it to your name or email; it is connected to you only if you choose to email us your Support ID) and receives your purchase and subscription history, along with technical information such as device model, iOS version, Application version, and IP address. We never receive your payment-card details. If you allow anonymous usage analytics, RevenueCat may also collect an Apple Search Ads attribution token through Apple's AdServices framework to measure whether an App Store ad led to the install (RevenueCat then stores the related Apple Search Ads campaign details with your anonymous App User ID); this does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

### Information You Send Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you write. The **Contact us** button pre-fills a **Support ID** (your anonymous RevenueCat ID and Firebase analytics ID) so we can locate or delete the related third-party records if you ask. You can remove it from the email before sending.

### What We Do Not Collect

- Your name, email address (unless you email us), phone number, or any account details. The Application has no account or login.
- Your precise location, contacts, photo library, camera, microphone, or calendar. (Saving the optional share card to Photos is add-only; the Application cannot read your photos.)
- Apple Health data. The Application does not read from or write to Apple Health.
- The advertising identifier (IDFA). The Application does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, shows no ads, and therefore does not display an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

## 2. Device Features and Permissions

- **Notifications**: reminders are **local notifications** scheduled on your device by iOS. They are not push notifications and involve no server. The Application asks for notification permission only after a purchase or when you turn on reminders in Settings. Reminders show only "Reps" above the text you chose, and the free-trial reminder (sent two days before a trial ends) uses neutral wording.
- **Face ID / passcode lock (optional)**: if you turn on **Lock Reps with Face ID**, iOS performs the check. Your biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and is never accessible to the Application or to us.
- **App-switcher cover (optional)**: hides the Application's screen in the iOS app switcher. It runs on your device only.
- **Audio and vibration cues**: session cues are generated and played on your device, including while the screen is locked. Nothing is recorded; the Application does not use the microphone.
- **Rating prompt**: the Application may show Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple handles any rating you leave.
- **Sharing**: when you finish the 8-week plan you can share a congratulation card through the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it, and the card does not include your training data. If you pick **Save Image** in the share sheet, iOS asks once for permission to add the card to your Photos; the Application can only add that image and cannot see your photo library.
- **Source links**: educational sources open the publisher's website in an in-app browser. Those websites are run by third parties under their own privacy policies.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

- provide the training program on your device (plan, sessions, cues, hold tests, streak, reminders);
- process and restore purchases (Apple, RevenueCat);
- find and fix crashes (Firebase Crashlytics);
- understand, in aggregate, how the Application is used so we can improve it, only if you allowed anonymous usage (Google Analytics for Firebase);
- answer your support requests.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles. Your training information is never used by us for any purpose, because we never receive it.

## 4. Legal Bases (EEA, UK, and similar laws)

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on: your **consent** for anonymous usage analytics and the optional AdServices attribution token (you can withdraw it any time in Settings › Privacy); our **legitimate interest** in keeping the Application stable for crash reports (you can turn them off in Settings › Privacy); and **performance of a contract** for processing your purchases.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Local data** stays on your device until you delete the Application (or restore your device without its backup).
- **Third-party data** (analytics events, crash reports, purchase records) is kept according to each provider's retention policy linked above. To request deletion, email us your Support ID (see Section 8).

## 7. Security

Your training information never leaves your device, so there is no server copy to be breached. Optional features add discretion on the device itself: the Face ID lock, the app-switcher cover, neutral reminder text, and a neutral home-screen name. Analytics and purchase identifiers are anonymous. No security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; please keep your device protected with a passcode.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

- **Analytics**: turn **Share anonymous usage** on or off in Settings › Privacy.
- **Crash reports**: turn **Send crash reports** on or off in Settings › Privacy.
- **Reminders**: turn them on or off, change their times and text in Settings › Reminders, or change notification permission in iOS Settings.
- **Local data**: delete the Application to remove all training data from your device.
- **Access or deletion of third-party records**: because there is no account, we cannot look you up by name. Copy your **Support ID** from **Settings › Safety & disclaimer › Support ID** (or use the Contact us button) and email it to [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software); we will ask RevenueCat and Google to access or delete the records linked to it, as far as those providers allow. Purchase records that Apple and RevenueCat must keep for accounting or legal reasons may be retained.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 9. Children's Privacy

If you believe a child has used the Application, please contact us at [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software) and we will take appropriate action.
