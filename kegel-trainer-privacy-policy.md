# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

Thank you for using **Reps** (store name on the App Store: "Kegel Exercises for Men: Reps"; on Google Play: "Kegel Workout for Men: Reps"; the "Application"). It covers both the iPhone version (App Store) and the Android version (Google Play). This Privacy Policy explains how NEXTLINE YAZILIM LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ ("Service Provider", "we", or "us") handles information when you use the Application.

The Application is a guided pelvic-floor (kegel) training program for men: a 12-week plan of short daily sessions with vibration, tone, or voice cues, step-by-step guides, a weekly self-timed hold test, and educational content linked to its sources. **There is no account, and your training information stays on your device.** We do not run a server that stores your data, and we never receive your goal, your answers, your hold times, or your training log.

## 1. Information We Handle

### Stored Locally on Your Device (never sent to us)

Everything you enter or create while training is saved only in the Application's local storage on your device. We never receive, see, or store any of it. This includes:

- **Your focus and setup answers**: the primary goal you pick, your experience level, your answer to the "Before you start" safety question, your training pace, and the daily moments you anchor your sessions to.
- **Training log**: each completed session (date and time, plan week and day, position, exercise counts, duration, the cue pack used, and whether the screen was locked).
- **Hold tests**: your self-timed hold results, the date of each test, and your weekly check-in answers (for example, how easy the muscle was to find and how the sessions felt).
- **Plan and streak**: your plan progress, streak, checkpoints, and graduation status.
- **Reminders and settings**: reminder times, the reminder text and emoji you choose, cue and sound settings, appearance, the app lock (Face ID on iPhone, fingerprint or face unlock on Android) and app-switcher cover settings, and your privacy choices.

This information lives only in the Application's on-device storage. Deleting the Application deletes it. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this local data may be included in that device backup according to your Apple settings; on Android, it may be included in your device's Google backup according to your Google settings. That backup is managed by Apple or Google, not by us, and is not a sync to our servers. Restoring a purchase brings back your subscription, not your training log.

### Anonymous Usage Analytics (Share anonymous usage stats)

The Application uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how it is used. It is controlled by the **Share anonymous usage stats** switch in **Settings › Privacy**:

- **Default:** the switch starts **off** if your device's region is set to a country in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland (or if the region cannot be determined), and **on** elsewhere. You can turn it on or off at any time.
- Turning it off stops collection and resets the analytics identifier on your device. If you chose "Not now" in an earlier version of the Application that asked, it stays off.

While it is on, the Application sends anonymous product events such as: which onboarding step was viewed, that a session started, was paused, or was completed, which cue pack was used, that a hold test was completed (not its result), which paywall was shown, the plan type selected (yearly or lifetime), whether a purchase or restore succeeded, whether you allowed reminders, and which Learn or how-to topic was opened. A plan status (free, trial, or Reps Plus) may be attached as a user property.

The Application uses a strict allowlist so that the following can **never** be sent to analytics: your primary goal, your safety-question answer, your hold times or test tiers, your check-in answers, your training log, or your reminder text.

Advertising features of Google Analytics are disabled: ad storage, ad personalization, and ad user-data signals are turned off, no advertising identifier is collected (no IDFA on iPhone; no Advertising ID and no Android ID on Android), and automatic screen reporting is disabled. As with any network service, Google may derive an approximate location (such as country or region) from your IP address.

### Crash Reports

If the Application crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report containing the stack trace, device model, operating system version (iOS or Android), Application version, a Firebase installation identifier, and the names of the last few in-app actions before the crash (action names only, with no values or training data). Crash reports are on by default and can be turned off in **Settings › Privacy › Send crash reports**.

### Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google through Google Play on Android, and tracked by RevenueCat. RevenueCat assigns a random, **anonymous App User ID** (or, on Android, one tied to an access code you were given). The Application never links this ID to your name or email; it is connected to you only if you choose to email us your Support ID. RevenueCat receives your purchase and subscription history, along with technical information such as device model, operating system version, Application version, and IP address. We never receive your payment-card details. On iPhone only, while anonymous usage analytics is on, RevenueCat may also collect an Apple Search Ads attribution token through Apple's AdServices framework to measure whether an App Store ad led to the install (RevenueCat then stores the related Apple Search Ads campaign details with your anonymous App User ID); this does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

### Information You Send Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you write. The **Contact us** button pre-fills a **Support ID** (your anonymous RevenueCat ID and Firebase analytics ID) so we can locate or delete the related third-party records if you ask. You can remove it from the email before sending.

### What We Do Not Collect

- Your name, email address (unless you email us), phone number, or any account details. The Application has no account or login.
- Your precise location, contacts, photo library, camera, microphone, or calendar. (Saving the optional share card to Photos is add-only; the Application cannot read your photos.)
- Health data from Apple Health or Health Connect. The Application does not read from or write to either.
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android). The Application does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and shows no ads, so it does not display an App Tracking Transparency prompt on iPhone. The Application contains no Facebook or Meta software and no advertising network.

## 2. Device Features and Permissions

- **Notifications**: reminders are **local notifications** scheduled on your device by iOS or Android. They are not push notifications and involve no server. The Application asks for notification permission only after you agree in a short reminder screen (shown once), after a purchase, or when you turn on reminders in Settings. Reminders show only "Reps" above the text you chose, and the free-trial reminder (sent two days before a trial ends) uses neutral wording.
- **App lock (optional)**: if you turn on the lock (Face ID or passcode on iPhone; fingerprint, face unlock or screen lock on Android), your phone's operating system performs the check. Your biometric data is handled entirely by the operating system and is never accessible to the Application or to us.
- **App-switcher cover (on by default; you can turn it off)**: hides the Application's screen in the app switcher (on Android, the recent-apps screen; on some Android versions it also blocks screenshots and screen recording while on). It runs on your device only.
- **Audio and vibration cues**: session cues are generated and played on your device, including while the screen is locked. Nothing is recorded; the Application does not use the microphone.
- **Rating prompt**: the Application may show the store's standard rating prompt (Apple on iPhone, Google Play on Android). The store handles any rating you leave.
- **Sharing**: when you finish the 12-week plan you can share a congratulation card through your phone's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it, and the card does not include your training data. You can also share a link to the Application from **Settings › Share Reps**. On iPhone, if you pick **Save Image** in the share sheet, iOS asks once for permission to add the card to your Photos; the Application can only add that image and cannot see your photo library.
- **Source links**: educational sources open the publisher's website in an in-app browser. Those websites are run by third parties under their own privacy policies.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

- provide the training program on your device (plan, sessions, cues, hold tests, streak, reminders);
- process and restore purchases (Apple, Google Play, RevenueCat);
- find and fix crashes (Firebase Crashlytics);
- understand, in aggregate, how the Application is used so we can improve it, while anonymous usage stats are on (Google Analytics for Firebase);
- answer your support requests.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles. Your training information is never used by us for any purpose, because we never receive it.

## 4. Legal Bases (EEA, UK, and similar laws)

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on: your **consent** for usage analytics (in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland it starts off and is enabled only if you switch it on; you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings › Privacy); our **legitimate interest** in keeping the Application stable for crash reports (you can turn them off in Settings › Privacy); and **performance of a contract** for processing your purchases.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Local data** stays on your device until you delete the Application (or restore your device without its backup).
- **Third-party data** (analytics events, crash reports, purchase records) is kept according to each provider's retention policy linked above. To request deletion, email us your Support ID (see Section 8).

## 7. Security

Your training information never leaves your device, so there is no server copy to be breached. Features on the device itself add discretion: the app lock, the app-switcher cover (on by default), neutral reminder text, and a neutral home-screen name. Analytics and purchase identifiers are anonymous. No security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; please keep your device protected with a passcode.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

- **Analytics**: turn **Share anonymous usage stats** on or off in Settings › Privacy.
- **Crash reports**: turn **Send crash reports** on or off in Settings › Privacy.
- **Reminders**: turn them on or off, change their times and text in Settings › Reminders, or change notification permission in your phone's settings.
- **Local data**: delete the Application to remove all training data from your device.
- **Access or deletion of third-party records**: because there is no account, we cannot look you up by name. Copy your **Support ID** from **Settings › Safety & disclaimer › Support ID** (or use the Contact us button) and email it to [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software); we will ask RevenueCat and Google to access or delete the records linked to it, as far as those providers allow. Purchase records that Apple, Google and RevenueCat must keep for accounting or legal reasons may be retained.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data-protection authority.
