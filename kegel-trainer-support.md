## Important: Reps Is Not Medical Advice

Reps is a fitness and wellness app for pelvic-floor exercise. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition, and it does not promise any result. Check with your doctor before starting if you have had prostate or pelvic surgery, have a catheter, have pelvic pain or trouble peeing, or have a neurological condition. **Stop if an exercise hurts**, and see a doctor or pelvic health physiotherapist if pain continues. In an emergency, call your local emergency number. You can read the full safety notes anytime in **Settings › Safety & disclaimer**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the plan work?

Reps gives you an 8-week plan with 3 short sessions a day. A plan day is a rolling 24-hour window that starts with your first session, so you can train at any time that suits you. Each week builds on the last, and a weekly hold test shows how your longest hold is changing. After 8 weeks you can switch to a lighter maintenance routine or repeat the plan at a harder level.

### I'm not sure I'm squeezing the right muscle.

Open the step-by-step guide from Today or from Learn. It walks you through finding the muscle and what you should and shouldn't feel. If finding it keeps being hard, a pelvic health physiotherapist can check it with you.

### Can I train with the screen off or while listening to music?

Yes. Choose a cue pack (vibration, tones, or voice) in **Settings › Cues & sound**. Tone and voice cues keep playing with the screen locked and through your earbuds, and Reps can mix with your music or lower it during a session. Use **Test cues** to check what you'll hear and feel.

### I don't feel the vibration or hear the cues.

- Check that the cue pack you want is selected in **Settings › Cues & sound** and run **Test cues**.
- Vibration works while the screen is on; with the screen locked, use tones or voice through earbuds or the speaker.
- Check your volume, and that Bluetooth earbuds are connected before you start.
- A phone call or another app taking over audio pauses the session; resume it when you're back.

### Something hurt during a session. What happens?

Tap **Something hurts?** in the session. The session ends and isn't counted, your streak day is protected, and the week repeats so you don't move on too fast. If the pain doesn't settle, see a doctor or pelvic health physiotherapist.

### What is the hold test?

It's a self-timed check of your longest hold that you start and stop yourself. It is for motivation and tracking your own progress, not a medical measurement.

### How do reminders work, and what do they show?

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone at your three session times, and only if that session isn't done yet. They show "Reps" above the neutral text you choose; words like "kegel" can't be used in reminder text. Set times, text, quiet mode, and the icon badge in **Settings › Reminders**. To hide the text completely on your lock screen, set **Show Previews** to "Never" for Reps in iOS Settings › Notifications.

### Can I keep Reps private on my phone?

Yes. In **Settings › Privacy** you can turn on **Lock Reps with Face ID** (or passcode) and the **app-switcher cover**. The home screen shows only "Reps" and its icon. Please don't use iOS "Hide App" for Reps: hidden apps can't send reminders.

### Where is my data stored? Do I need an account?

There's no account. Your goal, answers, hold times, and training log are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. Purchases go through Apple and RevenueCat. Anonymous app-usage events go to Firebase Analytics only if you allowed it, and crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics. You can change both in **Settings › Privacy**. See the Privacy Policy for details.

### Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Your training log moves with your device backup. If you set up a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, Reps' local data is normally restored with it (this is handled by Apple's backup, not by us). There is no cloud sync between devices. **Restore purchases** brings back Reps Plus, not your training log.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove all training data from your phone. To ask us to delete the anonymous analytics and purchase records held by our providers, copy your **Support ID** from **Settings › Safety & disclaimer** and email it to [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software). Without an account, that ID is the only way we can find your records.

### What's free, and what's in Reps Plus?

Free: onboarding, your first guided session, your day-1 hold test, the full plan to read, all Learn and how-to content, safety information, reminders, and privacy features. **Reps Plus** unlocks every session of the plan, the weekly hold tests, and the maintenance and repeat programs. You can choose **Yearly** (with a 7-day free trial if your Apple ID is eligible) or **Lifetime** (pay once). Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

### Will I be reminded before my free trial ends?

Yes. If notifications are on, Reps reminds you 2 days before your trial ends; if they're off, a banner on Today shows the end date. You can cancel any time during the trial.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open **Reps › Settings › Plan & subscription › Manage subscription**, or **iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions**. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Reps Plus until the end of the current period or trial. Nothing in your plan is deleted.

### I bought Lifetime but still have a yearly subscription.

Apple doesn't cancel a subscription automatically when you buy Lifetime. Cancel the yearly plan in **Manage subscription** so you aren't charged again.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Go to **Settings › Restore purchases** while signed in to the Apple ID you used to buy. If nothing is found, check that you're on the same Apple ID and connected to the internet.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### The paywall shows an error instead of prices.

Prices load live from the App Store. Check your internet connection and tap **Retry**. If it keeps failing, try again later or contact us.

### Does Reps work offline?

Yes. Sessions, cues, hold tests, your plan, Learn, and reminders all work offline. You need a connection only for purchases and restores, loading prices, opening source links, and sending crash or usage reports.

### Does Reps show ads or track me?

No. There are no ads, no ad SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites, so Reps doesn't show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

### Can people see Reps in my App Store purchases?

Yes. Apple shows the store name "Kegel Exercises for Men: Reps" on receipts and in your purchase history. The in-app purchase is named "Reps Plus".

### I found a bug or have an idea.

Email [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software) (or use **Settings › Contact us**) with a short description, your iOS version, and the app version shown in **Settings › Safety & disclaimer**. Screenshots help a lot.
