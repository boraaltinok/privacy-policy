## 2. Menu Content You Create

The Application is a menu builder. You provide content such as dish names, descriptions, prices, venue name and details, item photos, and an optional venue logo. This content is entered manually or produced by the AI import feature described in Section 3.

- **On your device.** Your menus are stored on your device (using Hive), and item photos and logos are saved in the Application's private storage.
- **Cloud sync and backup.** The text of your menus (dishes, sections, prices, venue details and settings) is also saved to our cloud database (Google Cloud Firestore), linked to your app user ID, so it can be backed up, published, translated, and restored. This data is accessible only to your app user ID and our backend. If you sign in with Apple or Google, your item photos and logo are also backed up to our cloud storage (Google Cloud Storage) so they can be restored on a new device.
- **Publishing is your choice.** When you choose to **publish** a menu, a copy of it (including its text, logo, item photos and any translations) is hosted on Google Firebase and served at a **public web address** (for example, `menu-maker-fb.web.app/...`). A published menu is **publicly accessible by design**: anyone who has the QR code or link can view it. Do not include information in a published menu that you do not want to be public.
- **Uploaded photos and logos.** Item photos and logos uploaded to our cloud storage (whether for publishing or for backup) are stored at long, unguessable web addresses that can be opened by anyone who has the exact address, so that your public QR menu can display them. They are not listed or discoverable otherwise.
- **You stay in control.** You decide whether to publish, what to publish, and when to update or unpublish a menu. Unpublishing or updating a menu changes what is served at the public address going forward.

## 3. Camera, Photos, and AI Features

### 3.1 Camera and Photo Library
With your permission, the Application uses your device's **camera** to scan a paper menu, and your **photo library** to select menu photos, item photos, and a venue logo. The Application only accesses the photos you choose.

### 3.2 AI Import and Translation (Remote Processing)
When you use **AI import**, the menu photos you capture or select are uploaded to our cloud storage and processed by our backend (Google Firebase Cloud Functions, region europe-west1). Our backend sends the photos to **OpenRouter**, an AI routing service, which passes them to a third-party AI model provider (currently Google's Gemini models) to extract the text into a structured menu. The extracted result is saved to your account so the Application can show it to you.

When you use the premium **translation** feature, your menu text is sent from our backend through OpenRouter to an AI model provider for translation, and the translations are saved with your menu.

This processing is performed **remotely**, not on your device. We do not send your name, email address, or account information to the AI providers — only the menu photos or menu text needed for the request.

**Retention of scans.** Menu photos uploaded for AI import are stored temporarily and deleted automatically within 24 hours, and immediately if you delete your account. The AI providers process the content under their own terms and privacy policies.

### 3.3 Accuracy Disclaimer
AI extraction and translation are automated and **may contain errors, omissions, or inaccuracies**. You are responsible for reviewing and correcting the results before using or publishing your menu.
