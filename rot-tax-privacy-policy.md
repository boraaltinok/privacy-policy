## 1. Information Collection and Use

### 1.1 Automatically Collected Information
When you download and use the Application, certain data may be automatically collected, such as:
- **Device operating system and version**
- **Pages/screens visited within the Application**
- **In-app interaction events** (e.g., onboarding progress, intercept and unlock events, rep counts, paywall views)
- **App performance and crash data**

We may use this automatically collected information for analytics, troubleshooting, and to improve the functionality of the Application.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Identifiers
The Application's third-party SDKs may generate and collect pseudonymous identifiers, including:
- **Analytics identifiers** (e.g., Amplitude device/user IDs)
- **Subscription identifiers** (e.g., RevenueCat anonymous app user IDs)
- **Crash reporting identifiers** (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics installation IDs)
- **A locally generated random app-install ID** (a UUID created on your device and used to associate analytics and subscription data)
- **Apple Search Ads attribution token** (an anonymized AdServices token that may be collected by the RevenueCat SDK to measure whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign; it does not identify you personally and does not involve cross-app tracking)

These identifiers are SDK-generated, pseudonymous, and not provided by you. They are used to associate usage data, manage subscriptions, and diagnose issues. Amplitude and RevenueCat may sync identifiers to link analytics data with subscription status.

### 1.3 No User-Provided Information
The Application does not require you to create an account or submit any personal information. There is no login, no email or password, and no profile. If you contact our support team, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and message contents.

## 2. Camera Data

The Application uses your device's camera to count your exercise repetitions (e.g., push-ups) using on-device body-pose detection. **All camera processing is performed entirely on your device.** The Application does not record, store, or transmit any photos, videos, or camera frames. Only the resulting rep counts and session outcomes (such as paid/unpaid status and duration) are stored locally on your device.

If the camera is unavailable or you choose the fallback mode, the Application can count reps using your device's **motion sensors** instead. Motion sensor data is likewise processed entirely on your device and never transmitted.

---

## 3. Shortcuts Automations and App-Open Events

The Application's core feature relies on **Shortcuts automations that you create yourself** on your device. When you open an app you have chosen to protect (e.g., TikTok, Instagram, X, Reddit, YouTube), your automation opens Rot Tax with a link indicating which protected app was opened. This information is processed on your device to run the intercept flow and is recorded in your local ledger. The Application does not monitor, read, or access the content of any other app, and it cannot see what you do inside other apps.

---

## 4. Locally Stored Data

The Application stores data on your device using local storage (Hive), including:
- **Daily ledger** (sessions, taxes paid, unlocks taken, dodges, and any outstanding debt)
- **Streak data** (current and best streak)
- **Tax configuration** (protected apps, base rate, exercise type)
- **App preferences** (notification settings, sound settings, onboarding progress)
- **Premium status cache**

A small summary (such as your streak and today's totals) may also be written to a shared container on your device so the optional home-screen widget can display it. All of this data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can delete it at any time by clearing app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application.

---

## 5. Notifications

The Application can send **local notifications** (such as an evening debt reminder or a weekly receipt notification). These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — the Application does not use push notifications and no notification data leaves your device. Notifications are optional and can be disabled in your device settings at any time.
