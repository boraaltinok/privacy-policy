# Rot Tax - Support

Welcome to the Rot Tax support page. We're here to help you get the most out of the Collector.

## Getting Started

Rot Tax makes opening your doomscrolling apps cost you push-ups. You set up a Shortcuts automation for each app you want to protect; when you open one, Rot Tax intercepts it and charges camera-counted reps to unlock the feed for a timed window. The app is available for iOS.

### Key Features
- **App Intercepts**: Your own Shortcuts automations redirect protected apps (TikTok, Instagram, X, Reddit, YouTube) to Rot Tax
- **Camera Rep Counting**: Push-ups counted on-device with body-pose detection (motion-sensor fallback available)
- **Escalating Tax**: The rep price rises with every unlock you take in a day, and resets at midnight
- **Streaks & Ledger**: A daily ledger of taxes paid, dodges, and debt — all stored on your device
- **Weekly Receipt**: A shareable summary of your week
- **Home-Screen Widget**: Streak and daily totals at a glance

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How do I get started with Rot Tax?**
A: Download the app, pick the apps you want to protect, set your base rate, and follow the guided setup to create the Shortcuts automations. A demo audit at the end of onboarding shows you exactly how an intercept works.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. Rot Tax is a local-first application — your ledger, streak, debt, and settings are stored on your device only and are not transmitted to our servers. There are no accounts and no cloud backup. Analytics and crash data sent to third-party services are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). For more information, refer to our [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/boraaltinok/privacy-policy/blob/main/rot-tax-privacy-policy.md).

**Q: Does Rot Tax record me while I do push-ups?**
A: No. The camera feed is analyzed on your device in real time to count reps. Nothing is recorded, saved, or uploaded — no photos, no videos, no frames.

### Shortcuts Automations

**Q: Opening TikTok/Instagram doesn't trigger Rot Tax. What should I do?**
A: Check the automation in the Shortcuts app:
1. Open Shortcuts > Automation and confirm an automation exists for that app
2. Make sure it is set to **Run Immediately** (not "Ask Before Running")
3. Confirm the automation opens Rot Tax
4. Re-run the automation test from Settings > Protected Apps

**Q: Can I bypass the intercept?**
A: Technically yes — the automations live on your device and you control them. Rot Tax is a motivational tool, not a parental-control lock. The streak and ledger exist to make skipping feel expensive.

**Q: How do I stop protecting an app?**
A: Remove it in Settings > Protected Apps, then delete the matching automation in the Shortcuts app.

### Camera & Counting

**Q: Why does Rot Tax need camera access?**
A: The camera is used to detect your body position and count exercise reps in real time. All processing happens on your device — nothing leaves your phone. You can grant or review the permission in Settings > Rot Tax > Camera.

**Q: Reps aren't counting or the tracker says "tracking lost." What should I do?**
A: Try the following:
1. Make sure your full upper body is visible in the camera frame
2. Prop the phone against a wall or object, roughly 1–2 meters away
3. Improve the lighting — dim rooms make pose detection harder
4. If the camera still struggles, use the motion-sensor fallback mode offered on the counting screen

**Q: How accurate is the rep counting?**
A: Counting is designed to be forgiving — it should almost never miss a genuine rep, and counts are approximate rather than a strict measure of form or athletic performance.

### Streaks, Debt & Notifications

**Q: What happens if I don't pay my tax?**
A: An unpaid session becomes debt for that day. If it's still unpaid at midnight, the debt is written off and your streak is reset. Debt never carries over to the next day.

**Q: Why did my streak reset?**
A: A day ended with unpaid debt, or no protected activity was resolved as paid. The streak obituary screen shows the details once after it happens.

**Q: Does Rot Tax send notifications?**
A: Only optional local notifications — an evening debt reminder and a weekly receipt. They are scheduled on your device (nothing is pushed from a server) and can be turned off in Settings or in your device's notification settings.

### Subscription & Premium

**Q: Is Rot Tax free?**
A: Yes — Rot Tax is currently completely free. Optional premium plans (monthly, yearly with a free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase) may be introduced later; prices are always shown in the app before any purchase.

**Q: How do I restore a purchase on a new device?**
A: Go to Settings > Restore Purchases. Purchases are linked to your App Store account and will be restored automatically.

**Q: How do I cancel a subscription?**
A: Subscriptions are managed through your App Store account. Go to your device's subscription settings to cancel — deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

### Technical Support

**Q: The app is not working properly. What should I do?**
A: Try these troubleshooting steps:
1. Close and reopen the app
2. Restart your device
3. Ensure you have the latest app version
4. Confirm that camera permission is granted
5. Re-check your Shortcuts automations are enabled and set to Run Immediately

**Q: The home-screen widget isn't updating.**
A: Remove and re-add the widget, then open Rot Tax once so it can refresh the shared data. Widgets are refreshed by the system and can lag a few minutes behind.

## Data Management

**Q: How do I delete my data?**
A: Your ledger, streak, and settings are stored locally on your device. You can delete them by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application. Data collected by third-party services (analytics, crash reports) is subject to their respective retention policies as described in our [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/boraaltinok/privacy-policy/blob/main/rot-tax-privacy-policy.md).

**Q: Can I share my weekly receipt?**
A: Yes — open the weekly receipt and use the share action to send the receipt image anywhere via your device's share sheet. Sharing is entirely user-initiated.
