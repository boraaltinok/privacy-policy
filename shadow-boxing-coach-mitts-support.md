## Train Safe

Shadows is a fitness app for boxing training at home. It is not medical advice and does not replace a coach or a doctor. Warm up before every round and clear some space around you. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially with a heart condition, high blood pressure, a recent injury, or if you are pregnant. **Stop if something hurts**: in a round, tap **Pause › Something hurts?**. If you feel chest pain, faintness, or trouble breathing, stop and call your local emergency number. The full safety note is in **Settings › Safety**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does a coached round work?

The coach teaches a combo, shows it slowly, has you repeat it, extends it, speeds it up, and then makes you cover and move. The board shows one combo at a time, with the current pad lit. Use **Slower** or the speed and difficulty dials any time; they're free in every round. Every Start builds a fresh round.

### Is the coach a real person?

No. It's a generated coach voice. The cues are written the way pad-holders actually call pads.

### Why names, not numbers?

Names tell you what to throw without learning a code first. Prefer numbers? Switch in **Settings › Voice**.

### I'm a southpaw.

Set your stance in **Settings › Training profile**. Moves that depend on your side flip for you.

### Does it work with my music and with the screen locked?

Yes. Shadows plays over your music and never pauses it. Turn on **Lower music in rounds** in **Settings › Sound & music** if you want your music quieter, and use the coach/music slider there to balance them. You can lock your screen; the coach, pad sounds, and bells keep playing.

### I can't hear the coach or the pads.

- Check your volume and that your earbuds or speaker are connected before you start.
- Check **Settings › Sound & music** (pad sounds on, coach level up).
- A phone call or another app taking over audio pauses the round; resume it when you're back.
- If it still fails, restart the app and email us your iOS or Android version and app version (**Settings › About**).

### What does "Vibrate with pads" do?

It adds a tap on each pad sound, useful with your phone in your pocket. It works while the screen is on.

### Which languages does the coach speak?

The coach speaks English today. Other languages use English move names until their voice is ready. Pick yours in **Settings › Coach language**.

### What's free, and what's in Shadows Coach?

Free: the round timer, every move sheet with Hear it, the cheat sheet, the dials, reminders, and **one full coached workout**. **Shadows Coach** unlocks every coached workout after your free one, every program day, your custom workouts, and Try it on the move sheets. You can choose **Weekly**, **Yearly** (with a 7-day free trial if your Apple ID or Google account is eligible), or **Lifetime** (pay once). Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

### I reinstalled the app and my free workout didn't come back.

The free coached workout is a one-time allowance on your device, and the count can survive reinstalling. The timer, move sheets, and cheat sheet stay free.

### Will I be reminded before my free trial ends?

Yes, if notifications are on: Shadows reminds you two days before your free week ends. You can cancel any time during the trial.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open **Settings › Plan › Manage subscription**, or **iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions** on iPhone, or **Google Play › profile picture › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions** on Android. Cancel at least a day before it renews. Deleting the app does not cancel. You keep Shadows Coach until the end of the current period, and nothing is deleted.

### I bought Lifetime but still have a subscription.

Apple and Google Play don't cancel a subscription automatically when you buy Lifetime. Cancel it in **Manage subscription** so you aren't charged again.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Tap **Restore** on any paywall, or go to **Settings › Plan › Restore purchases**, while signed in to the Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. If nothing is found, check that you're on the same Apple ID or Google account and connected to the internet.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. App Store: request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Google Play: follow [Google Play's refund steps](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). If you get stuck, write to us and we will help you with the steps.

### The paywall shows an error instead of prices.

Prices load live from the App Store or Google Play. Check your internet connection and tap **Retry**. If it keeps failing, try again later or contact us.

### How do reminders work?

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone for the days and time you pick in **Settings › Reminders**. Shadows asks for notification permission only when reminders are offered (never at launch). You can turn them off there or in your phone's notification settings. On Android a reminder may arrive up to about an hour after the time you picked.

### Does Shadows work offline?

Yes. Coached rounds, the timer, move sheets, the cheat sheet, programs, and reminders all work offline. You need a connection only for purchases, restores, loading prices, opening the Terms and Privacy links, and sending usage data or crash reports if you have them on.

### Where is my training data? Do I need an account?

There's no account. Your workouts stay on your phone: your training profile, round history, programs, and custom workouts are stored on your phone and never uploaded. Purchases go through Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android), and RevenueCat. Usage data goes to Firebase Analytics and crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics only while their switches in **Settings › About** are on (they start off in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland). See the Privacy Policy for details.

### Can I move my history to a new phone?

Your local data moves with your device backup: if you set up a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, or a new Android phone from your Google backup, Shadows' data is normally restored with it (handled by Apple or Google, not by us). There is no cloud sync. **Restore purchases** brings back Shadows Coach, not your round history.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove your training data from your phone. To ask us to delete the purchase records held by RevenueCat, copy your **Support ID** from **Settings › About** and email it to [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software). Without an account, that ID is the only way we can find your records. To stop usage data and crash reports, turn off their switches in **Settings › About**.

### Does Shadows show ads or track me?

No. There are no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites, so Shadows doesn't show an App Tracking Transparency prompt, and the Android version does not use the advertising ID.

### Can I share the cheat sheet?

Yes. Open the cheat sheet in Learn and use the share button to send it as an image through your phone's share sheet.

### I found a bug or have an idea.

Email [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software) with a short description, your iOS or Android version, and the app version shown in **Settings › About**. Screenshots help a lot.
