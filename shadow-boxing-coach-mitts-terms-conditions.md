## 1. The Application

The Application is an audio-first shadow-boxing coach for training at home. A coach voice teaches combos, repeats them, speeds them up, and calls defense and footwork while pad sounds mark the tempo. It also offers a round timer, move sheets, a boxing cheat sheet, short programs, custom workouts, and reminders. The Application has no user accounts, and your training data is stored only on your device.

## 2. Health and Safety: Not Medical Advice

**Please read this section carefully.**

- **Fitness only.** The Application is a fitness app for boxing training. It is **not a medical device**, does not give medical advice, and does not replace a qualified coach or a doctor.
- **Talk to a doctor first** before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, a recent injury, or are pregnant, or if you are unsure whether boxing training is right for you.
- **Warm up and train within your limits.** Warm up before every round. Make sure you have enough clear space around you, a safe floor, and suitable footwear. Use the speed and difficulty controls and **Slower** to match the pace to your fitness.
- **Stop if something hurts.** Put your hands down, rest, and see a doctor if pain keeps coming back, swells, or gets worse. In a round, **Pause › Something hurts?** ends the round for this reason.
- **Emergencies.** If you feel chest pain, faintness, or trouble breathing, stop and call your local emergency number. Do not rely on the Application in an emergency.
- **No promised results.** We do not promise or guarantee any fitness, skill, weight, or health outcome. Results vary from person to person.
- **Training aid, not a measurement.** The Application calls moves and keeps time; it does not watch you, count your punches, or check your technique.
