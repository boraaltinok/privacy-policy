# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

Thank you for using **Song Mixer** ("Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how NEXTLINE YAZILIM LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ ("Service Provider", "we", or "us") handles information when you use our Application.

The Application lets you put the vocals of one song over the beat of another and turn the result into a mashup and a short video. **Songs and videos you bring into the Application are separated, analyzed, mixed, and rendered entirely on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.** The Application has no user accounts and no backend server of its own.

## 1. Information We Handle

### Stored Locally on Your Device (not transmitted to us)

The Application keeps the following on your device only:

- **Imported songs**: copies of the audio files you import, the sound taken from videos you pick, and the song's title and artist as read from the file's own metadata (or its file name).
- **Analysis results**: data the Application computes from your songs on your device, such as tempo, beats, key, sections, and separated vocal and beat layers.
- **Mashups (projects)**: the songs paired, your mix settings and adjustments, the name, feeling, and optional cover photo you choose, and whether a mashup is saved.
- **Rendered files**: share videos and exported audio or layer files you create, kept temporarily until you save or share them.
- **Preferences and counters**: onboarding status, session count, rating-prompt timing, and similar app settings.
- **Free listening meter**: the number of seconds of free listening you have used and whether you have seen the first-run upgrade screen. These two values are kept in the iOS Keychain, so they may remain on your device even if you delete and reinstall the Application.

This data lives in the Application's local storage on your device. Deleting a song or mashup in the Library removes it; uninstalling the Application removes the rest (except the Keychain values noted above). If iCloud Backup is enabled on your device, your local Application data, including imported songs and mashups, may be included in your iCloud Backup according to your Apple settings. That backup is managed by Apple; it is not a sync to our servers.

### Content You Choose to Bring In

You decide which songs, videos, and photos the Application can use. The Application only receives the items you pick:

- **Files**: audio or video files you choose with the iOS Files picker, or open in the Application from another app.
- **Videos from Photos**: videos you pick with the iOS photo picker; the Application uses their sound.
- **Music library**: songs you own that are stored on your device, chosen with the iOS music picker. Protected streaming songs cannot be opened.
- **Share Extension**: an audio or video file you send to the Application from another app's share sheet. It is passed to the Application through a container shared only between the Application and its own extension on your device.
- **Cover photo**: a single photo you pick for a mashup's cover.

None of these items, their audio, their titles, or their analysis results are sent to us or to any third party.

### Information Automatically Collected by Third-Party SDKs

Some technical information is collected by two integrated third-party SDKs. **Your songs, videos, photos, song titles, file names, and mashup names are never sent to these SDKs.**

- **Crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)**: when the Application crashes or hits an error, a report is sent that includes the device model, iOS version, Application version, stack trace, and a Firebase installation identifier. Reports also include a short log of recent in-app actions in metadata form, for example "import started (source: files)", "paywall viewed (reason: meter)", "song analyzed (tempo, number of sections)", or an error code. These logs are designed to exclude audio content, song titles, and file names.
- **Subscription status (RevenueCat)**: an anonymous app user identifier generated by RevenueCat, your purchase and subscription history from the App Store, and basic device and Application information, used to unlock Premium and restore purchases.
- **Network-level data** such as IP address may be observed by these SDKs as part of normal operation.

The Application does **not** collect usage analytics and contains no analytics or advertising SDK. The Application does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not request or access the advertising identifier (IDFA).

We do not collect:

- your name, email address, phone number, or other contact information (unless you email us; see Section 9);
- your precise or approximate location;
- your contacts, calendar, health data, or microphone or camera input;
- any biometric data;
- any photo, video, or song you did not pick yourself.

The Application displays no third-party advertisements.

## 2. Permissions

iOS asks for your permission before the Application can use the following. Each one is optional, and the rest of the Application keeps working if you decline.

- **Media & Apple Music**: lets you pick songs you own from your music library.
- **Photos (add only)**: lets the Application save your mashup videos to your photo library when you choose to save them.
- **Photos (picker)**: videos and cover photos are chosen through the iOS photo picker, which shares only the items you select.
- **Notifications**: asked only after you start a free trial, to schedule one local reminder before your trial ends. The reminder is scheduled on your device; no push notification service is used.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

- provide the mixing features (import, analysis, matching, playback, saving, and video rendering), entirely on your device;
- process subscription purchases and restore prior purchases through RevenueCat;
- diagnose crashes and errors and improve stability through Firebase Crashlytics;
- show a local trial-ending reminder, if you allowed notifications;
- ask, at most once in a while, for an App Store rating through Apple's native rating prompt.

We do not sell your information. We do not use your content for advertising. We do not build user profiles. We do not use your songs to train any model.

## 4. Sharing and Exports

When you share a mashup video, export audio, or export layers, the Application creates the file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you send it (for example, a messaging app, social network, AirDrop, or Files). The app or service you choose then handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. When you save a video to Photos, it is added to your photo library on your device.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Application integrates with the following third parties. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how it handles data on its side:

- [Firebase Crashlytics (Google)](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- [Apple App Store, in-app purchases, and rating prompt (governed by Apple's policies)](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

The Application does not send your content to any AI service or cloud processing provider. Song separation and analysis run on your device.

## 6. Data Retention

**Local data**: stays on your device until you delete it in the Library or uninstall the Application. The free listening meter in the iOS Keychain may remain after uninstalling.

**Third-party data**: retention is governed by each provider's policy linked above. To request deletion of crash or subscription records tied to your anonymous identifier, contact us at [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software).

## 7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data we handle:

- your songs, videos, photos, and mashups stay on your device; there is no server of ours that holds them;
- the subscription identifier is anonymous (RevenueCat);
- crash reports are limited to technical diagnostics, not your content.

No security system is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
