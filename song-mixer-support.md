## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I make a mashup?

Add a **vocal** (the song you want to hear sung) and a **beat** (the song you want to hear underneath), then tap **Do the Magic**. Song Mixer finds a spot where the two fit and plays the result. Tap **Try another** for a different version, or use **Fix it** to adjust it. When you like it, save it with a name, a feeling, and an optional cover photo.

### Where can I bring songs from?

- **From a video**: saved TikToks, Reels, screen recordings, or any video in your Photos. Song Mixer uses the video's sound.
- **Files**: audio or video files in the Files app, iCloud Drive, or other file providers.
- **Music library**: songs you own that are downloaded to your device.
- **Share sheet**: in another app, tap Share and choose Song Mixer.
- **Demo sounds**: bundled vocals and beats made for trying things out.

See **Settings → How to bring songs** for step-by-step tips.

### Why can't I open a song from Apple Music or Spotify?

Streaming songs are copy-protected and can only be played inside their own app, so iOS does not let other apps open them. Use songs you own as files, songs you bought and downloaded, or the sound from a video.

### Can I use any song I want?

Only use audio you own or have permission to use. Song Mixer is a tool and does not give you any rights to the songs you mix. Before you post a mashup publicly, make sure you have the rights holders' permission or that your use is allowed where you live. Platforms you post to may also mute or remove content under their own copyright rules. See the Terms & Conditions, Section 3.

### Are my songs uploaded anywhere?

No. Separating, analyzing, matching, playing, and rendering all happen on your iPhone. Your songs, videos, photos, and mashups are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Files leave your device only when you share or save them yourself.

### Why does the app ask for permissions?

- **Media & Apple Music**: only to pick songs from your music library. If you decline, you can still use videos and Files.
- **Photos (add only)**: only to save your mashup videos to your photo library.
- **Notifications**: only after you start a free trial, to remind you before it ends.

You can change these anytime in **iOS Settings → Song Mixer**.

### Why does the mix sound imperfect sometimes?

Song Mixer separates vocals and beats automatically on your device. With some songs, parts of instruments can stay in the vocal, or the timing may need a nudge. Try another pair, tap **Try another**, or use **Fix it** to adjust. Clear vocals and steady beats work best.

### Why did playback stop? What is free?

On the free plan you can import, make, fix, save, and share 15-second videos of your mashups. Listening to your own mashups uses a free listening meter. When it runs out, playback fades out and stays locked until you go Premium. Demo playback during onboarding does not count.

Premium unlocks unlimited listening, full-length video and audio export, Studio, and layer export. Plans and prices are shown on the upgrade screen.

### How does the free trial work?

If a free trial is available to your Apple ID, the upgrade screen shows it, with the day it ends and the price after that. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends in **iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** to avoid being charged. If you allow notifications, Song Mixer reminds you about two days before the trial ends.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Open **Settings → Restore purchases** in the app. You must be signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. A network connection is required.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open **Settings → Manage subscription** in the app, or **iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Premium until the end of the current period. Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Will my mashups disappear if my subscription ends?

No. Saved mashups stay in your Library on your device. Free-plan limits then apply again.

### How do I share or save a video?

Open a mashup and tap **Share**. Choose the length, then share it to any app through the iOS share sheet, or save it to Photos.

### Does the app work offline?

Yes. Importing, analysis, mixing, playback, saving, and video rendering all work offline. You only need a connection to see plans, purchase, restore, and check your subscription.

### How do I delete my data?

In the **Library**, delete songs or mashups you no longer want. Deleting a song also deletes the mashups that use it. Uninstalling Song Mixer removes all songs and mashups from your device. If iCloud Backup is on, older backups may still contain them until they are replaced.

### Can I move my mashups to a new iPhone?

There is no account or cloud sync. Your data moves with an iCloud or computer backup restored to the new device.

### I found a bug or have a feature request.

Email [support@nextline.software](mailto:support@nextline.software) with a short description, your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the app version (shown in **Settings → Version**). Screenshots help a lot. Please don't attach copyrighted songs.
